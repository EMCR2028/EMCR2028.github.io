About the congress

A focused forum for corrosion electrochemistry.

The Electrochemical Methods in Corrosion Research (EMCR) symposia are established international meetings devoted to the development, understanding and application of electrochemical methods in corrosion science.

The 2028 edition follows EMCR 2025 in Venice. The scientific programme will combine invited lectures, oral communications, poster sessions and informal discussion, continuing the EMCR tradition of close interaction between academia, research institutes and industry.

Scientific scope

Conference topics

The programme will cover established and emerging electrochemical approaches across the full corrosion research spectrum.

01

Electrochemical methods

Advanced methods, experimental design and interpretation in corrosion research.

02

Coupled techniques

Integration of electrochemistry with spectroscopy, microscopy and surface analysis.

03

Local electrochemistry

Scanning, mapping and spatially resolved electrochemical techniques.

04

Sensors & monitoring

Electrochemical sensors, in-situ monitoring and condition assessment.

05

Passivity & localisation

Passivation, breakdown, pitting, crevice and galvanic corrosion.

06

Mechanisms & modelling

Mechanistic interpretation, numerical simulation and predictive modelling.

07

Inhibitors & coatings

Corrosion inhibitors, conversion layers, anodizing and protective coatings.

08

Cathodic protection

Fundamentals, monitoring, design and field applications.

09

Energy & transport

Corrosion in batteries, hydrogen, transport and energy-conversion systems.

10

Industrial environments

Infrastructure, concrete, marine, oil & gas, electronics and biomedical systems.

Vigo · Galicia

Science beside the Atlantic.

Vigo is a maritime city on the Ría de Vigo in northwest Spain. Its Atlantic setting, strong research environment and proximity to the Cíes Islands make it a distinctive location for EMCR'2028.
